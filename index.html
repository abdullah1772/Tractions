Choose seven days, a month, or a custom range. Compare periods and see how your views, followers, and engagement change.

The dashboard runs locally. Connected account credentials and imported analytics stay in your local installation. Export your metrics and back up your history.

CONNECTED WITH YOUR PERMISSION

Your accounts. Your choice.

Tractions reads analytics from accounts you authorize through official platform APIs. Instagram and Facebook connect independently. YouTube uses Google authorization. TikTok integration is being developed and tested in Sandbox; it is not yet available as a live connection.

Available metrics depend on each platform, account, and permission. Missing values stay unavailable, and sample illustrations are kept separate from real data. The public website does not display your private dashboard.
