Privacy Policy

Tractions is a local-first creator analytics application maintained by Abdullah, the owner of the GitHub account abdullah1772. This policy describes the local application and this public information website.

1. What the application accesses

When you connect an account, Tractions requests the access you authorize through that platform. Depending on the integration and permissions, this can include account IDs and profile names, follower or subscriber counts, published post and video metadata, and available performance metrics such as views, likes, comments counts, shares, reach, saves and watch time. The application also stores topic tags, format labels, notes and preferences you enter.

Tractions uses access tokens and, where applicable, refresh tokens and developer client credentials to call official platform APIs. The application does not ask you for your social account password. Sign-in occurs through the platform's authorization flow, or you supply an existing developer access token to your own local installation.

2. Why the information is used

Account information and metrics are used to display your analytics, compare date ranges, retain performance history, show content details, support your annotations, and provide exports and backups. Authorization credentials are used to retrieve permitted account data and renew access where the integration supports it. Tractions does not use your connected-account data for advertising, selling data, or training artificial intelligence models.

3. Where information is stored

The current dashboard runs on your computer. Its database, annotations, account metadata and encrypted credentials are stored in the local app folder. A local encryption key is stored separately in that folder. Someone able to read both the database and the key may be able to decrypt credentials; keep the folder and your computer protected.

The application communicates directly with the connected platforms to obtain data. It does not send your analytics or tokens to a Tractions-operated cloud dashboard. CSV exports and normal JSON backups exclude access tokens, refresh tokens, developer secrets and the local encryption key. They still contain personal analytics and annotations, so keep them private.

4. Platform services and integration status

Instagram, Facebook and YouTube integrations operate according to your permissions and the relevant platform's own policies. TikTok integration is currently in development and Sandbox testing, not generally available as a live connection. Once enabled, it is intended to read your authorized profile statistics and public-video metadata and engagement counts for the same local analytics purposes described here. Connecting is optional, and unsupported metrics remain unavailable.

Requests to platform APIs may disclose technical information such as your IP address to those platforms. Content thumbnails may be loaded from platform-hosted servers. Each platform controls its own service, logs, authorization records and retention.

5. Retention, disconnection and deletion

Imported history is retained locally until you delete it. Disconnecting an account inside the app removes its stored local authorization credentials while retaining imported analytics and notes. Disconnecting locally does not necessarily revoke the app's authorization at the platform.

To remove all locally stored application data:

  1. Disconnect accounts in the app and revoke their authorization in the relevant platform's account settings.
  2. Stop the local application.
  3. Delete the app's local data folder, including its database and encryption key. This removes local analytics, notes, preferences and stored credentials.
  4. Delete any exported files, backups and other copies you no longer want to retain.

Data held independently by GitHub or a social platform is governed by that provider; deleting local data does not delete a social account or content published on it.

6. This public website

This site provides product information, these terms and this policy. It does not provide a social sign-in form or receive your local dashboard's credentials. The site does not include advertising, analytics scripts or a Tractions tracking-cookie system. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub handles site requests under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting external links is subject to the destination's policies.

7. Support information

If you contact the maintainer or submit a GitHub issue, the information you send is used to respond and investigate the request. GitHub issues are public. Do not include tokens, client secrets, passwords, personal analytics or other private information in an issue. Use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile for a sensitive request.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The updated date appears at the top. Material changes to data handling should be reflected here before the changed behavior is released.

For questions or requests about information actually received by the maintainer, visit the contact page. Local dashboard data ordinarily remains on your own device and can be deleted there.
